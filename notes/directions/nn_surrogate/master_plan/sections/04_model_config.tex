\section{Model configuration}
\label{sec:modelconfig}
%-----------------------------------------------------------------------

An idealised single-basin ocean spanning pole to pole, with an adjoint generated
by Tapenade.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}Y{0.45}Y{1.55}@{}}
\toprule
Grid & $51\times198\times36 = 363{,}528$ cells; 36 levels reaching
  $4600\,\mathrm{m}$\\
Timestep & $1800\,\mathrm{s}$; 27 MPI ranks\\
Spin-up & 200 model-years, complete. All $2{,}400$ restart states retained, one
  every 30.5 days\\
Adjoint window & 5 years. The forward sweep stores model states; the reverse
  sweep propagates sensitivity backwards, writing fields every 5 days for 366
  records\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Grid detail.}
Of the $363{,}528$ cells, $332{,}274$ ($91.4\,\%$) are wet, and $9{,}627$ of
$10{,}098$ surface cells are ocean. The year is 366 days.

\paragraph{Cost function.}
$J$ is the upper-cell AMOC transport at 26.05\dg N: meridional volume transport
across a full-width zonal section, integrated over the upper $982\,\mathrm{m}$,
in Sverdrups, over the upper $k\le25$ levels at $i\in[1,51]$, $j=127$.
\code{ALLOW\_COST\_ATLANTIC\_HEAT\_DOMASS} is defined, so it is mass transport
rather than heat transport despite the routine's name. It is evaluated as a
30-day average at the end of the forward
sweep, which suppresses high-frequency variability. Every sensitivity discussed
below is $\partial J/\partial(\text{control})$ for that metric.

\paragraph{Controls.}
One adjoint run returns sensitivities to all controls at once: initial
temperature and salinity, vertical diffusivity, wind stress, and the surface
heat, shortwave and freshwater fluxes. No separate experiment is needed per
control.

\paragraph{Reference run.}
One adjoint run is complete and analysed, covering model years 2180 to 2185
(SLURM job 28486). It serves as the experimental control, for the reason given in
Section~\ref{sec:leg}.
